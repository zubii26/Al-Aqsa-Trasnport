Frequently Asked Questions

How much luggage can each vehicle carry?

Our sedans (Camry) comfortably carry 2 large bags, the GMC Yukon carries 4–5, and the Toyota Hiace can carry up to 10. For larger groups, the Toyota Coaster carries 15+ bags.

Is there a vehicle suitable for elderly pilgrims?

Yes — our GMC Yukon offers easy step-in access and our drivers provide door-to-door assistance, including luggage support for elderly or less mobile passengers.

What if our group is slightly over the seat capacity?

Contact us via WhatsApp before booking — we can recommend the next vehicle size up or split your group across two vehicles at no extra coordination cost.

Do your drivers speak English?

Yes, our drivers are multilingual and experienced in serving international pilgrims. Mention your preferred language when booking via WhatsApp.
